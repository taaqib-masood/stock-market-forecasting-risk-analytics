🛡️ Drawdown Guard — "Why I de-risked"

        
The passive overlay scales basket exposure by how far Nifty is below its recent peak — and explains every move.

      


      

        

          
          −4%
        

        
        
0% (peak)−8% de-risk−20% floor−35%

        

      


      

        
🟢 Above −8% — fully invested
Ordinary dips aren't worth timing. Stay 100% in the basket so you never miss the snap-back recoveries that punish market-timers.

        
🟡 −8% to −20% — de-risking
Exposure scales down linearly as the drawdown deepens. Each step to cash is a graded response, not a panic switch — avoiding binary-rule whipsaw.

        
🔴 Below −20% — 30% floor
Catastrophic-crash mode: cut to the 30% floor as insurance. Never zero — long-only, and calling the exact bottom is a loser's game.
